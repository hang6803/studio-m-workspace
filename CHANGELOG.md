# Changelog

- 0.9.11 — The app no longer ships a shared API key. Pages and the Mac build take `M_API_URL` only. After the PIN, MApp.gs checks the session token. Sign-in, register, first PIN, PIN reset and the team list are rate limited. Saving or deleting a job, and saving or deleting a shoot, need a signed-in role (member and above). Paste the new `api/MApp.gs` and redeploy before the new Pages build goes live; then delete the old `API_KEY` script property and the GitHub secret `M_API_KEY`. Code.gs is unchanged.
- 0.9.9 — Ask M handles a pasted schedule (a WhatsApp list of many events): every shoot in the reply becomes its own card, in order, with an "Add all N to Calendar" button; each card remembers on its own whether it was added. M is told to put every event in one reply (team crew only, matching tags like @JHang or "Mun (Studio M)"; times, roles, outside crew and gear go in notes). If a long reply is cut off inside a card, the app asks M for the rest by itself (up to 3 times). MApp.gs: the AI reply limit goes from 1200 to 4000 tokens (live after the next Apps Script deploy).
- 0.9.8 — Phone: changing page no longer stretches the tab bar up the screen (the press ripple inside a tab was made part of the layout by a later style, so for a moment the tab bar grew to most of the screen and the page above went black; the bar is now also capped at its own height), and a tapped tab or link shows no blue box. Computer: 小M on Team (from the profile button) no longer twitches; his glide after landing counted as a new jump, which lifted him and set him down again in a loop. Only his own jumps lift him now, not a blink on his face.
- 0.9.7 — A wrong PIN no longer goes to the loading screen. The PIN is checked right on the PIN screen (the dots ripple in a wave); a wrong one flashes the dots red, shakes them, buzzes and shows the reason in red. The loading only comes once the PIN is right. 小M lands where he should: the lift that keeps him above the top bar measured him mid-jump, so he landed off his spot and snapped back (Backup, Drives); now he lands on it, takes one more look once he is down (room above his head on Backup, a page that finished drawing under him) and glides there instead of snapping. On Team he no longer vanishes when the cards redraw. Phones no longer use View Transitions for page changes (Android squashed the screen: tab bar halfway up, page black); the new page rises in from a soft blur instead. Backup: Add backup sits at the right of the filter row, and the sort is an icon whose arrows turn over (on a phone Add backup is a round +).
- 0.9.6 — On a computer, pop-ups are dialogs in the middle of the screen instead of drawers from the bottom: they rise in from a soft blur with a little spring and sink back the same way, the page behind goes out of focus, and there is no grab handle or drag-to-close. 小M hops onto the dialog's top edge once it has landed and hops straight back to his spot as it closes. Phones keep the drawer.
- 0.9.5 — After the PIN, the Studio M loading stays up until Home is whole: the data is in and the graph is drawn, settled, with every dot there. Then the loading breathes out onto the finished graph, which takes one small zoom-out breath, instead of opening on black and the graph popping in after. Waits 8 s at most.
- 0.9.4 — M learns from every answer the AI gives (on this device: what kind of request it was, places from the shoots it set up), so with no internet or when the AI is down M still answers from the data on the phone: what isn't backed up (for a person, a drive or a year), shoots this week / next week / on a date, which drive a job is on, and a shoot card from plain words like "12月5日到7日 在 Ipoh 拍 Lim 婚礼" (saving still needs the server). Answers link straight to the job or the day. Settings → AI model shows how much M has learned. 小M is lifted to the top layer while he jumps, so the top bar no longer cuts him off. MApp.gs has its own getJob (Code.gs has none; without it the whole script failed to load).
- 0.9.3 — The mouse lamp is smaller (300px across instead of 760px), so it lights just what you point at.
- 0.9.2 — Real light instead of grey: a warm key light from the top right falls across the app (blacks stay black, lit things glow), and a lamp follows the mouse; on a phone, light flashes where your finger lands and fades. The film grain and vignette now sit behind the panels, so nothing in front (小M included) looks washed out. The tab you are on is lit from above (a thin neon strip on the bar and a cone of light), the sidebar's current page is a lit pill that glows onto the bar, and pressing a bar item blooms light from your finger. 小M has a warm back light and light bouncing off the floor under him, with a soft contact shadow.
- 0.9.1 — Signing in is a 6-digit PIN (no name to pick). Under the pad: First time (on the team, no PIN yet), Register (new: name, Gmail, birthday, PIN; waits until an admin, M or the boss approves it) and Forgot PIN (an admin, M or the boss types their PIN to let you pick a new one). An old 4-digit PIN still opens once with OK and then asks for a 6-digit one. Roles: boss > M > admin > member. The Team page shows sign-up requests with Approve and Reject, everyone grouped by role, a role picker for the boss and M, Remove only where you outrank the person (nobody removes the boss; only the boss removes M), and an activity log (admins and above) of sign-ins, sign-ups, approvals, removals, PIN resets and who opened the log, each with the IP and device it came from. Wrong PINs lock only that name for 15 minutes, so one stranger can't lock the whole team out. Team: each person's card holds their own settings behind Edit (birthday, role, a new PIN set by an admin, M or the boss, Remove), with an Add card at the end. Texture: finer steps between greys with a trace of warmth, a light film grain and vignette, every entrance comes into focus out of a soft blur, fields sink in gently with a deeper shadow when you type, numbers keep their width, paragraphs get an easier measure, presses squash and bounce back, toggles wobble once, and drags snap into place (a shoot onto its day, the chat window to the screen edge, a drawer back up). Ask M answers with DeepSeek through the studio's own server (MApp.gs), so the key stays there and phones need no setup; it is the default AI on the phone and the web (Settings → AI model → DeepSeek (Studio M)). M's replies in the chat wrap and read normally again (a Backup tag style was squeezing them into one line). Sounds are the Apps Script Backup's: water-drop notes, a breath of air for opening and closing, a click on every press and a soft gear tick as you scroll; haptics are about twice as strong (on iPhone, the bigger moments roll two or three ticks). M's blink is one smooth motion now: crouch, leap and spin into the burst, then pop out, arc and land with two small bounces, each step with its own easing; the copy of him that used to fly off is gone (less for the phone to draw) and the burst has fewer scraps. Team leaves the tab bar and the sidebar: tap your avatar (top right on a phone, bottom left on a computer) for your profile and the team; Log out sits right next to it. The super account is called M now (it was 小M), and the chat box is one calm field again (no box inside a box).
